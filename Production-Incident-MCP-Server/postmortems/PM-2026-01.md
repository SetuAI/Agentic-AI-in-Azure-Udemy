
# PM-2026-01 · Loan disbursements rejected because beneficiary accounts were frozen

- Date: 2026-01-09
- Services: payments, loan-service
- Severity: SEV-3
- Duration: not an outage (single customers)

## Summary

Several loan disbursements failed with "beneficiary account is frozen (code BNK-417)". Support first treated it as a payments bug.

## Impact

12 customers over two weeks had their loan money held. Each loan was moved to ON_HOLD automatically.

## Root cause

Not a bug. Code BNK-417 comes from the customer's own bank: the destination account is frozen, usually because the customer's KYC documents have expired. Payments correctly refused to send money to a frozen account.

## What fixed it

The operations team contacted each customer, who updated their KYC with their bank. The loans were then released from ON_HOLD and disbursed again.

## Lessons and follow-ups

- BNK-417 now creates an operations ticket automatically, instead of an engineering alert.
- The customer receives an SMS asking them to contact their bank.
- Support runbook updated: BNK-417 is a customer-side issue, not a system failure.
