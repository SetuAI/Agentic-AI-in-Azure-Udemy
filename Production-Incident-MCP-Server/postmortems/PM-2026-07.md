
# PM-2026-07 · Wrong amount shown in EMI confirmation SMS

- Date: 2026-07-25
- Services: notifications
- Severity: SEV-3
- Duration: 6 hours

## Summary

EMI confirmation SMS messages showed the amount in paise instead of rupees (for example "Rs 1240000" instead of "Rs 12,400").

## Impact

About 9,000 customers received a confusing SMS. No money was affected.

## Root cause

Release notifications v1.6.0 updated the SMS template and passed the amount without converting it from paise to rupees.

## What fixed it

Release notifications v1.6.1 converted the amount correctly. A follow-up SMS apologised to the affected customers.

## Lessons and follow-ups

- SMS templates now have tests that check the amount format.
