
# PM-2026-05 · OTP requests blocked by a wrong rate limit on api-gateway

- Date: 2026-05-17
- Services: api-gateway, notifications
- Severity: SEV-2
- Duration: 38 minutes

## Summary

Customers asking for an OTP got "429 Too Many Requests" from api-gateway, even on their first try.

## Impact

About 2,300 OTP requests were refused between 18:02 and 18:40.

## Root cause

Release api-gateway v2.8.0 changed the rate limit for /otp/send from 100 per minute per customer to 100 per minute for ALL customers together, because of a typo in the configuration.

## What fixed it

Rolling back api-gateway to v2.7.9. A corrected version was released the next day.

## Lessons and follow-ups

- Rate limit changes are now tested with many customers at once, not one.
- api-gateway configuration changes need a second reviewer.
