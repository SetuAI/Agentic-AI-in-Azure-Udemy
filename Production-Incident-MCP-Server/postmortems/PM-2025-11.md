
# PM-2025-11 · OTP SMS messages not delivered during QuickSMS outage

- Date: 2025-11-21
- Services: notifications, api-gateway
- Severity: SEV-2
- Duration: 1 hour 35 minutes

## Summary

Customers could not log in or confirm payments because OTP SMS messages were not delivered. Our outside SMS provider, QuickSMS, was returning errors to every request.

## Impact

About 4,000 OTP messages failed between 11:10 and 12:45. Logins and payment confirmations that need an OTP failed for that time.

## Root cause

QuickSMS had an outage on their side and returned "503 Service Unavailable" for every message. No change had been released on our side; our notifications service was working correctly but had no second provider to fall back to.

## What fixed it

QuickSMS restored their service at 12:45. On our side, nothing could be done during the outage, because notifications had only one SMS provider.

## Lessons and follow-ups

- Add a second SMS provider (SwiftSMS) and switch to it automatically when QuickSMS returns 503 for more than 2 minutes.
- Show customers an in-app message during SMS outages, instead of a silent failure.
- When notifications errors appear with no release in the same window, check the SMS provider's status page first.
