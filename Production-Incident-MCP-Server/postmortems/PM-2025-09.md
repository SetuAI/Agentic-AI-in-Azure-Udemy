
# PM-2025-09 · Loan applications slowed down by credit bureau timeouts

- Date: 2025-09-03
- Services: loan-service
- Severity: SEV-3
- Duration: 3 hours

## Summary

Loan applications took much longer than usual, and some failed with "Credit bureau did not reply within 5 seconds".

## Impact

About 8% of loan applications failed between 10:00 and 13:00 and had to be submitted again.

## Root cause

The credit bureau's own system was slow that morning. A small number of these timeouts happen every day and are normal; that day the number was ten times higher.

## What fixed it

The credit bureau recovered on their own. We added an automatic retry, so a single timeout no longer fails the application.

## Lessons and follow-ups

- A few credit bureau timeouts per hour are normal background noise and need no action.
- Alert only when they are more than 5 times the usual hourly rate.
